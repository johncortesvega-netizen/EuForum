# Concept

European Public Square is a concept for a free, slower multilingual forum for Europe.

It is not a feed, not a Reddit clone, and not an engagement platform. It uses old-school forum structure: categories, rooms, threads, replies, latest activity, searchable context, and visible discussion history.

## Core idea

People write in their own language. Other people read in their own language. The original remains visible.

Translation is a bridge, not a replacement for the original.

The forum should support context before reaction:

- rooms instead of feeds;
- threads instead of viral fragments;
- replies instead of rage loops;
- original-language preservation;
- careful prompts;
- posting limits;
- human moderation;
- evidence review;
- visible receipts.

## Sydney Protocol role

The Sydney Protocol layer is used only to clarify:

- evidence gaps;
- pressure language;
- missing context;
- dignity risks;
- translation caveats;
- escalation risks;
- repair questions.

It does not judge truth, punish users, rank posts, censor content, or make enforcement decisions.

## Privacy posture

The post is the print. The platform should not create a hidden behavioral profile, ad graph, interest model, political profile, or invisible second record of the user.

## Pace posture

The forum should be slower by design: no infinite feed, no engagement farming, no streaks, posting limits against spam and addiction loops.

## Funding posture

The forum should remain free to use. Optional donations may support server, security, maintenance, translation, moderation, and infrastructure costs. Donation and spending receipts should be visible.

Donations must not buy influence over speech, ranking, moderation, governance, protocol behavior, or visibility.
