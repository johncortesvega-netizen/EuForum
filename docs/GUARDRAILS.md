# Guardrails

## Sydney Protocol rule

Clarify only.

The Sydney Protocol layer may show prompts such as:

- strong claim: ask for evidence;
- pressure language: clarify whether disagreement is possible;
- translation caveat: view original before judging tone;
- dignity risk: clarify who is meant;
- repair option: rephrase before escalation.

It must not:

- decide truth;
- call a person corrupt;
- rank users;
- punish users;
- censor language;
- certify legitimacy;
- replace moderators, fact-checkers, courts, institutions, or human judgment.

## Human roles

Moderators enforce hard boundaries and room rules.

Evidence reviewers add source and context notes.

Users speak, question, clarify, appeal, and repair.

## Hard boundaries

Direct harm, doxxing, child exploitation, targeted harassment campaigns, and illegal/safety-critical content require separate moderation rules and legal review. The protocol prompt layer is not the enforcement layer.
