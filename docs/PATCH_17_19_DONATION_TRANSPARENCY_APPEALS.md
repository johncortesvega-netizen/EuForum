# Patch 17+19 - Donation Transparency + Appeal Flow v1

Patch 17+19 adds a mock donation transparency page and a first local appeal flow for moderation visibility actions.

## Added

- Donation transparency page.
- Monthly server-cost table.
- Mock monthly transparency summary:
  - donations received;
  - total spent;
  - reserve.
- Public spending receipt model.
- Spending receipts rendered through the existing receipt cards.
- Clear boundary that donations buy no influence.
- Appeal button for appealable visibility actions.
- Appeal status display.
- Appeal receipts.
- Appeal progression:
  - submitted;
  - under review;
  - resolved.

## Boundaries

Donations may cover operating costs only. Donations do not buy ranking, speech privilege, moderation privilege, visibility, appeal priority, protocol influence, or governance control.

Appeals challenge human moderation visibility actions. Appeals are visible, receipted, and separate from Sydney Protocol clarification. Sydney Protocol still does not enforce.

## Still not added

- No real donations.
- No payment processor.
- No donor account model.
- No production appeal assignment.
- No legal case-management workflow.
- No automatic moderation or automated appeal decisions.
