# Private Alpha Admin Checklist

Use this before each private alpha testing session.

## Access

- Confirm the invite list is limited.
- Confirm testers know the prototype is not public.
- Confirm no payment/donation processor is enabled.
- Confirm no production identity provider is enabled.
- Confirm test accounts can be removed after the session.

## Forum State

- Confirm rooms are limited and understandable.
- Confirm posting limits are visible.
- Confirm receipts render on posts.
- Confirm the receipts ledger opens.
- Confirm the donation transparency page says donations buy no influence.
- Confirm known limitations are linked or shared with testers.

## Review Operations

- Assign at least one moderator.
- Assign at least one evidence reviewer.
- Confirm report flow works.
- Confirm moderator action receipts appear.
- Confirm two-moderator review language is visible.
- Confirm appeal flow works for temporary visibility actions.

## Safety

- Have a stop plan for direct harm or illegal content.
- Keep private tester contact details outside the prototype.
- Do not import production personal data.
- Do not treat Sydney Protocol prompts as moderation decisions.

## Session Close

- Save alpha feedback separately from hidden user profiles.
- Record issues and decisions.
- Remove test data if required.
- Decide whether another invite round is safe.
