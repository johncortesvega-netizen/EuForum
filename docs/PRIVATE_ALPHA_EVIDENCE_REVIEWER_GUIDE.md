# Private Alpha Evidence Reviewer Guide

Evidence reviewers add context. They do not decide truth.

## Reviewer Role

- Add source/context notes.
- Mark missing source context.
- Mark translation nuance.
- Mark legal or historical context.
- Mark claim scope.
- Attach visible evidence-note receipts.

## Do Not

- Decide who is right.
- Decide guilt, corruption, legitimacy, or political truth.
- Replace journalists, courts, moderators, or democratic institutions.
- Hide posts.
- Rank users.

## Good Evidence Notes

Good notes are:

- short;
- source-aware;
- careful about uncertainty;
- attached to a visible post;
- receipted;
- clear about what they do not decide.

## Boundary

Evidence reviewers add source/context notes only. They do not decide truth, guilt, corruption, or legitimacy.
