## Usability

- Could you understand how to post?
- Did the posting pause/limit feel fair?
- Could you understand name + country accountability?
- Did receipts help or feel too heavy?
- Could you find the original-language context?

## Trust And Safety

- Were Sydney Protocol prompts clearly non-judgmental?
- Were moderation boundaries understandable?
- Did the appeal route make sense?
- Did evidence notes feel helpful?
- Did donation transparency make donor influence boundaries clear?

## Open Feedback

- What confused you?
- What felt trustworthy?
- What felt too heavy?
- What should be removed before another alpha round?
- What must be fixed before any public launch?

## Consent Boundary

Feedback should improve the prototype. It must not become a hidden behavioral profile.
