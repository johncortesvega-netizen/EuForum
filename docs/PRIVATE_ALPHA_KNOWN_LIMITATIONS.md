# Private Alpha Known Limitations

This prototype is a private alpha candidate, not a public launch.

## Product Limitations

- No production authentication provider.
- No persistent browser token storage.
- No real translation provider.
- No real donation or payment processing.
- No production moderation case management.
- No production appeal assignment.
- No notification system.
- No email verification.
- No rate-limit enforcement beyond the demo layer.

## Operational Limitations

- Moderation roles are demo-mode.
- Evidence review is local/demo-mode.
- Spending receipts are mock receipts.
- Donation transparency is a model, not accounting software.
- Private alpha feedback must be collected carefully outside hidden profiling.

## Safety Limitations

- The prototype is not ready for open public registration.
- It is not ready for high-risk crisis moderation.
- It is not ready for legal compliance claims.
- It is not a replacement for courts, journalists, public institutions, or emergency services.

## Boundary

Ready for controlled invited testing does not mean ready for public launch.
