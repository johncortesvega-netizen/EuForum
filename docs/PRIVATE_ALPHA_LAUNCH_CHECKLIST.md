# Private Alpha Launch Checklist

This checklist gates a controlled invited alpha.

## Must Pass

- Setup guide reviewed.
- Admin checklist reviewed.
- Moderator guide reviewed.
- Evidence reviewer guide reviewed.
- Privacy and rules page reviewed.
- Known limitations shared with testers.
- Feedback form ready.
- No real donation/payment processor enabled.
- No public registration enabled.
- No production identity verification enabled.
- No automated moderation claims.
- Receipts ledger visible.
- Transparency page visible.
- Appeal flow visible.

## Invite Scope

- 20-50 invited users maximum.
- 3-5 rooms maximum.
- 2-3 languages maximum.
- Named point person for moderation.
- Named point person for evidence review.
- Clear stop condition for safety issues.

## Stop Conditions

Pause the alpha if:

- direct harm cannot be handled safely;
- testers misunderstand Sydney Protocol as enforcement;
- appeals cannot be handled by humans;
- privacy expectations are unclear;
- donation transparency is misunderstood as donor influence;
- moderation workload exceeds available humans.

## Launch Principle

Slow launch for a slow forum.
