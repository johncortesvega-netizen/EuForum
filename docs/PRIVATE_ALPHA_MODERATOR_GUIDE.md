# Private Alpha Moderator Guide

Moderators handle hard boundaries and room rules. Sydney Protocol prompts do not moderate.

## Moderator Role

- Review user reports.
- Check EU hard-boundary categories.
- Apply temporary visibility limits only when needed.
- Record visible moderation receipts.
- Route serious actions to two additional moderators.
- Keep appeals available where legally and safely possible.

## Do Not

- Treat reports as proof.
- Treat Sydney Protocol prompts as enforcement.
- Decide political truth, guilt, corruption, or legitimacy.
- Give donors, admins, friends, or loud users special treatment.
- Hide disagreement merely because it is difficult or unpopular.

## Serious Actions

Serious visibility actions require:

- named moderator;
- country shown for accountability;
- reason category;
- receipt;
- two-moderator review;
- appeal path where safe and lawful.

## Core Line

The platform does not erase disagreement. It restricts direct harm.
