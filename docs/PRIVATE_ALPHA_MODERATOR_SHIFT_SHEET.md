## Before Session

- Smoke test passed.
- Known limitations shared.
- Stop conditions reviewed.
- Appeal route reviewed.
- Direct-harm response plan reviewed.

## During Session

Track:

- reports received;
- posts temporarily hidden;
- two-moderator reviews;
- appeals submitted;
- evidence notes added;
- safety concerns;
- tester confusion.

## After Session

- Close open review items.
- Record unresolved appeals.
- Record high-severity issues.
- Decide whether another session is safe.

## Boundary

Moderators decide visibility under hard-boundary rules. Sydney Protocol prompts do not moderate.
