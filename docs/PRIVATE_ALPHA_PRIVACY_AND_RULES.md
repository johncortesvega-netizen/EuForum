# Private Alpha Privacy And Rules

## Privacy

The post is the print. No hidden second profile.

The private alpha may use:

- display name;
- country shown on posts;
- optional photo/avatar;
- account/login identifier;
- post and reply content;
- timestamps;
- room/thread structure;
- language metadata;
- visible receipts;
- moderation and evidence-review records.

The private alpha must not create:

- behavioral ad profiles;
- political profiles;
- hidden engagement scores;
- hidden personalization profiles;
- unnecessary identity-document storage;
- donor influence records;
- invisible second versions of users.

## Rules

- Speak as a person, not as a manipulation engine.
- Ask for evidence without attacking the person.
- Keep original-language context visible.
- Do not post direct threats, doxxing, child exploitation, illegal harmful instructions, or targeted harassment campaigns.
- Do not spam or flood rooms.
- Do not use donations, status, or relationships to seek moderation privilege.
- Appeals are allowed for visibility actions where legally and safely possible.

## Boundary

Name and country for accountability. Private life protected.
