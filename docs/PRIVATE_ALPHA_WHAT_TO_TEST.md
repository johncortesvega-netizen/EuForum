# Private Alpha What To Test

Use this checklist during the first invited alpha session.

## Core Forum

- Can you find a room?
- Can you understand a thread?
- Can you create a post?
- Can you understand the posting pause?
- Can you see the post receipt?

## Identity

- Is name + country accountability clear?
- Does optional photo feel optional?
- Is the privacy boundary understandable?

## Translation

- Is original-language preservation clear?
- Is translation shown as a bridge, not a replacement?

## Sydney Protocol

- Do prompts feel clarifying rather than judging?
- Is it clear that prompts do not moderate, hide, rank, or punish?

## Moderation And Appeals

- Can you report a post?
- Can you understand human review status?
- Can you understand two-moderator review?
- Can you submit or understand an appeal?

## Evidence Notes

- Do evidence/context notes help?
- Is it clear they do not decide truth?

## Donations And Transparency

- Is the transparency page understandable?
- Is it clear donations buy no influence?

## Alpha Pack

- Can you find setup, limitations, feedback, and launch checklist docs?

## Decision

After testing, choose one:

- safe to repeat with another small group;
- fix issues before another small group;
- pause the alpha.
