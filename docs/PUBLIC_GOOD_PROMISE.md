# Public Good Promise

European Public Square should remain free to use because a public square should not require payment to speak.

## The promise

- The public forum remains free.
- No ad targeting.
- No paid ranking.
- No donor speech privilege.
- No donor moderation privilege.
- No hidden profile economy.
- No algorithmic rage feed.

## Why donations may exist

Servers, domains, security, backups, translation, maintenance, and human review can cost money. Donations may help keep the square online, but donations must not buy power over speech, moderation, visibility, ranking, or protocol rules.

## Boundary phrase

Donations keep the lights on. They do not buy influence.
