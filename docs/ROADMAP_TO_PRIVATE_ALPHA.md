# Roadmap to Private Alpha

The goal is not a viral launch. The goal is a small, credible, testable forum that proves the public-square model.

## Pre-alpha requirements

- stable local prototype;
- backend MVP;
- minimal login;
- accountable identity display: name + country, photo optional;
- no hidden profile;
- posting limits;
- Sydney Protocol clarify-only prompts;
- visible receipts;
- human review pipeline;
- evidence reviewer notes;
- donation transparency mock;
- privacy and public promise pages;
- basic security review.

## Private alpha scope

- 20–50 invited users;
- 3–5 rooms;
- 2–3 languages;
- limited registration;
- no algorithmic feed;
- human moderation;
- visible receipts;
- donation page may be mock-only until real costs exist.

## Alpha questions

- Do people understand name + country accountability?
- Do posting limits feel fair?
- Do receipts increase trust or feel too heavy?
- Do Sydney Protocol prompts clarify without judging?
- Does translation as bridge make sense?
- Are moderation boundaries clear?

## Launch principle

Slow launch for a slow forum.
