# What This Is Not

European Public Square should be defined as much by what it refuses as by what it builds.

## It is not

- not an AI moderation system;
- a truth engine;
- a corruption detector;
- a propaganda detector;
- a Reddit clone;
- a rage-ranking feed;
- an ad platform;
- a hidden profiling system;
- a government identity database;
- a censorship machine;
- a replacement for courts, journalists, moderators, or democratic institutions.

## Sydney Protocol boundary

Sydney Protocol prompts clarify only. They may surface pressure, missing evidence, translation caveats, dignity risk, escalation risk, or repair questions. They do not judge, rank, punish, censor, decide truth, or enforce.

## Moderation boundary

Human moderators handle hard boundaries and direct-harm cases. Serious actions should be receipted and appealable.
