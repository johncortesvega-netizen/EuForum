European Public Square

A slower multilingual forum for context before reaction.

local prototype v0.18 / patch 22 first invited alpha session kit

Old-school forum structure, modern translation, careful prompts.

Write in your own language. Read in yours. Open the original whenever translation may miss tone. European hard-boundary categories define the direct-harm floor. The Sydney Protocol layer handles THRESHOLD pressure by clarification only: evidence gaps, pressure, dignity risks, missing context, and repair questions. The forum is free by design; donations may later cover operating costs with public spending receipts.

The post is the print No hidden profile, no ad graph, no invisible second version of the user.
Limits by design Cooldowns and posting limits reduce spam, rage loops, and addictive behavior.
Clarify only The Sydney Protocol does not judge, rank, punish, censor, hide posts, or decide truth.

Account login

Register or log in with display name + country. Tokens stay in page memory for this prototype.

Translation layer

Every translated post keeps a visible route back to the original language.

Translated text is a bridge, not a replacement for the original.

Sydney Protocol prompts

Prompts do not decide truth. They clarify what may need context, evidence, or repair.

Clarification layer, not enforcement layer.

Human roles

  • Moderators: enforce hard boundaries and room rules.
  • Evidence reviewers: add source/context notes, not truth verdicts.
  • Users: speak, question, clarify, appeal, and repair.

Posting limits

This shell models a session-only limit: 3 post attempts per 30 minutes. It is shown as product behavior, not backend enforcement.

EU hard-boundary map

Direct harm and illegal-content categories route to human moderation. Sydney Protocol THRESHOLD signals clarify only.

Human review queue

Reports trigger human review. Human moderators decide visibility. Serious actions require two-moderator review.

Evidence review

Evidence reviewers add source/context notes. They do not decide truth, guilt, corruption, or legitimacy.

Receipt ledger

Visible local receipts for prompts, evidence notes, and forum actions. No hidden second profile.

Funding model

Free public forum. Optional donations may later cover server, security, translation, maintenance, and human-review costs.

Donations keep the lights on. They do not buy influence.

Private alpha pack

Setup, runbook, invite, onboarding, test checklist, issue log, shift sheet, report, and go/no-go docs for invited testing.
